## Why This List Exists

Small service businesses live and die by discovery. Airbnb opens the door, but reviews stack slowly — a great provider can be one review-nudge away from a wave of bookings, and one silent month away from throwing in the towel.

If you book any of these folks and they take good care of you, please leave them a review. It costs you 90 seconds and it's the single most valuable thing you can do for them.
